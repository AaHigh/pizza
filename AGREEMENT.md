BLOCK PARTY PIZZA COMPETITION, COOK AGREEMENT

1. Cooking is free. Ovens are provided. Ingredients are provided free by the nonprofit organization. A cook invests time only.
2. Cooks are paid $5 per pizza cooked, for labor, independently of any prizes.
3. Category ingredient budgets are maximum ingredient costs per pizza, not prices: Cheese $5, Pepperoni $6, Vegetarian $6, Meat Lovers $7, Supreme $8. Meat Lovers is a proposed value for confirmation. Eaters always pay $5 all you can eat.
4. A competition pizza must stay within its category budget. Exceeding the budget is not playing fair, and the pizza is not eligible for competition.
5. Cooks may make any size pizza within the budget. Target size is 14 to 16 inches, undecided. Size versus ingredient quality is part of the competition: within the same budget, a bigger pizza or finer ingredients is a choice judges may reward either way.
6. Eaters and ingredient-bringers contribute at least $5. Cash, listed ingredients, or both.
7. The app publishes the necessary ingredients list with a value for each item. Ingredients you bring are credited at their listed value against your $5. Bring less than $5 worth, add cash for the difference. Bring $5 or more worth, you are covered.
8. To qualify as a cook, you must cook a real pizza at the event, from the provided ingredients. A pizza that is not genuine does not qualify. Entering the judged competition is optional.
9. Before the event, cooks post their ingredient lists online. Anyone who signs up to bring a listed ingredient gets that value credited. What helpers bring goes to the organization for the cooks.
10. The books are balanced. Nobody takes a profit. Eater money covers ingredients and labor first. Any money left after costs goes to the cooks as prizes.
11. The judged competition is a separate dimension on top, using the BetCube paradigm. Before you begin building a pizza, you declare in the app whether that pizza is a competition entry and how much you stake on it. Each pizza is an independent choice: you may enter none, one, or any number of the pizzas you make, at any amount per pizza. Once committed, the stake is binding and must be followed through.
12. The stake sets the size of the prize you can win. Skill decides who wins. A bigger stake does not buy a better score.
13. There is no cap on prizes.
14. Categories: Pepperoni, Vegetarian, Meat Lovers, Supreme, Cheese. Cheese is broad: cheese pizza, cheesy bread, cheese dips and dipping sauces all qualify.
15. Dough may be made from scratch or bought. Piemonte's Italian Delicatessen dough is listed in the app at its real price as the known neighborhood baseline. Scratch dough is budgeted at its flour, water, and yeast cost. A cook using scratch dough may use the savings elsewhere within the category budget.
