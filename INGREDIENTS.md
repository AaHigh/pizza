# Necessary ingredients list (template)

The app publishes this list with a value per item. Helpers sign up to
bring listed items; the value is credited against their $5. Cooks post
their lists before the event. Fill in real prices before publishing.

## Category ingredient budgets (maximum cost per pizza)

- Cheese: $5
- Pepperoni: $6
- Vegetarian: $6
- Meat Lovers: $8
- Supreme: $8

## Dough baseline

- Piemonte's Italian Delicatessen dough: $2.20 placeholder, to confirm
  (Piemonte's Italian Delicatessen, 616 E Olive Ave, Fresno).
- Scratch dough: flour, water, yeast cost TBD.

## Ingredients by category (values TBD)

- Cheese: mozzarella, sauce, dough, oil, salt.
- Pepperoni: pepperoni, mozzarella, sauce, dough, oil, salt.
- Vegetarian: vegetables, mozzarella, sauce, dough, oil, salt.
- Meat Lovers: meats, mozzarella, sauce, dough, oil, salt.
- Supreme: meats, vegetables, mozzarella, sauce, dough, oil, salt.

## Rules

- A helper's brought items are credited at listed value against the $5.
- A cook's competition pizza must stay within its category budget.
- The list is the single source of values. If it is not on the list, it
  has no credited value until it is added.
