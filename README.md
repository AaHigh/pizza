# Block Party Pizza Competition

South Tower District, Fresno. A nonprofit community pizza event and judged
skill competition. One facet of a multidimensional skill platform: the
platform carries the value, not any single game. Pizza proves the same rails
as the game facets: verified skill, voluntary stake, real economic
constraint, and reputation that compounds with practice. Long-term goal: in
10 years the best pizza cooks in America come out of the Tower District,
because practice under constraint builds that skill.

## The one price

Eaters pay **$5, all you can eat**. That is the only price in the event.

## Base layer (nonprofit, balanced books)

- Cooking is free. Ovens are provided by Aaron. Ingredients are provided
  free by the nonprofit organization. A cook invests time only.
- Cooks are paid **$5 per pizza cooked**, for labor, independently of any
  prizes.
- Eaters and ingredient-bringers contribute at least **$5 net**: cash,
  listed ingredients, or both.
- The app publishes the necessary ingredients list with a value per item.
  Ingredients brought are credited at listed value against the $5. Bring
  less than $5 worth, add cash for the difference. Bring $5 or more worth,
  you are covered.
- Before the event, cooks post their ingredient lists online. Anyone who
  signs up to bring a listed ingredient gets that value credited. What
  helpers bring goes to the organization for the cooks.
- Eater money covers ingredients and labor first. Any money left after
  costs goes to the cooks as prizes. Nobody takes a profit.
- The $5 works because the average eater does not eat a whole pizza.

## Competition layer (optional, on top)

- Entering the judged competition is optional. The base event works
  without it, the same way free play works without prize play.
- Category ingredient budgets are **maximum ingredient costs per pizza,
  not prices**. A competition pizza must stay within its category budget.
  Exceeding the budget is not playing fair, and the pizza is not eligible.
- Cooks may make any size pizza within the budget. Target size is 14 to
  16 inches, undecided. Size versus ingredient quality is part of the
  competition: within the same budget, a bigger pizza or finer ingredients
  is a choice judges may reward either way.
- Judged competition uses the BetCube paradigm. Before you begin building
  a pizza, you declare in the app whether that pizza is a competition entry
  and how much you stake on it. Each pizza is an independent choice: you
  may enter none, one, or any number of the pizzas you make, at any amount
  per pizza. Once committed, the stake is binding and must be followed
  through.
- The stake sets the size of the prize you can win. Skill decides who
  wins. A bigger stake does not buy a better score. There is no cap on
  prizes.

## Categories

Pepperoni, Vegetarian, Meat Lovers, Supreme, Cheese. Cheese is broad:
cheese pizza, cheesy bread, cheese dips and dipping sauces all qualify.

## Dough

Dough may be made from scratch or bought. Piemonte's Italian Delicatessen
dough is listed in the app at its real price as the known neighborhood
baseline (Piemonte's Italian Delicatessen, 616 E Olive Ave, Fresno).
Scratch dough is budgeted at its flour, water, and yeast cost. A cook using
scratch dough may use the savings elsewhere within the category budget.
A cook can win on recipe alone, and scratch dough can beat store-bought
dough.
