How it works

You order, the pie is assembled to order, baked to order in about a minute, and served immediately. Nothing is cooked in advance, nothing is held for later sale, and food left at the end of the day is disposed of.

Assemble

Dough, sauce, cheese, topping, built on the cart when you order it.

Bake

About a minute in the Ooni Karu 2 Pro, on propane, at high heat.

Serve

Straight off the cart, hot, right away. That is the whole service.

Where to find the cart

The cart is a non-motorized pushcart, pushed about half a mile from home to the Tower Theatre area, and pushed home after. It is stored at home after the county evaluates the storage site.

Hours and exact spot are posted in the High Tower District group when the cart is out. Early evening, not late night.

Done the lawful way

This is a Compact Mobile Food Operation (CMFO): limited food preparation only, one operator, no employees. The county packet was received November 6, 2026 from Fresno County Environmental Health, and the permit application is in progress. Nothing here is sold until the permit is in hand.

  • Plan check first: construction plans go to the county for review and approval before the cart is built or bought. Review takes up to 20 business days.
  • No dough made on the cart, and no dough made at home. Dough balls come from a permitted food facility, with the agreement filed before the first sale.
  • Everything on the cart, only on the cart: all food is held, stored, displayed, and served from the cart. No outside tables, no outside cooking.
  • Commissary: the cart operates from a permitted commissary, for water, wastewater, cleaning, and storage, serviced at least every day it is used.
  • On the cart: commercial-grade equipment, mechanical refrigeration at 41°F or below, a handwashing sink, soap and paper towels, a 10 BC fire extinguisher, and a first-aid kit. Propane mounted to the cart.
  • Food handler card: the operator holds a California Food Handler Card.
What the cart does not do: no fresh produce, no raw meat, no sausage, no thawing, no cooling cooked food, no washing food, and no cooking for later use. That is what keeps it a limited-preparation operation.

Also: the Block Party Pizza Competition

Separate from selling, and not the same money: the Block Party Pizza Competition is a community event in the South Tower District, $5 a head, all you can eat, nonprofit-style, no profit kept. Cooks eat free, ingredients are free to cooks, and the judged competition is optional, with a stake declared before a pizza is built. Categories are Cheese $5, Pepperoni $6, Vegetarian $6, Meat Lovers $8, Supreme $8, as maximum ingredient budgets per pizza, not prices.
