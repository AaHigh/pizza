Compact Mobile Food Operation - Simple Operating Plan
Aaron Hightower, Fresno, California

Operator
- One person operation. No employees.

Operation type
- Compact Mobile Food Operation (CMFO)
- Non-motorized pushcart, stored at my residence after evaluation
- Oven: Ooni Karu 2 Pro, propane
- Cart is pushed about half a mile to the Tower Theatre area and pushed home after service

Menu - keep it simple
- Whole pies only, $10 each. No slices sold separately.
- Ingredients per pie capped at $2.50.
- Offerings: cheese, pepperoni, plus a rotating offering, for a total of three to four offerings per night.
- No fresh produce.
- No raw meat. No sausage.

Dough
- Dough is NOT made on the cart.
- Dough is NOT made at home.
- Dough balls come from a permitted food facility. The written agreement for that facility will be filed before first sale.

Toppings and ingredients
- All ingredients from an approved source.
- Pre-sliced pepperoni, shredded cheese, canned sauce.
- No fresh produce to wash or slice on the cart.

Process on the cart
- Assemble to order.
- Bake to order, about one minute per pie.
- Serve immediately.
- No advance cooking. No hot holding for later sale. No cooling, thawing, or washing food on the cart.
- Food is disposed of at the end of the operating day.

Equipment and safety
- Overhead protection.
- Mechanical refrigeration at 41 degrees Fahrenheit or below.
- Handwashing sink.
- Access to a permitted commissary for dough preparation, storage, and cleaning. The commissary agreement will be filed before first sale.

Fees budgeted
- CMFO permit: $283 per year
- CMFO storage site evaluation: $229
- Food handler card: about $10
- City business tax certificate application: $14
- Seller's permit: free
- Total budgeted: $536

Please confirm in writing that this total covers this exact operation, and whether a separate plan check fee applies to a CMFO of this type, before I pay any additional fee.
